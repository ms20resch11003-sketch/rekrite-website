Bacterial Cellulose Hydrogel

Bacterial Cellulose Hydrogel is a high-purity, water-rich nanofibrous material engineered for versatile applications across healthcare, personal care, advanced materials, and sustainable product development. Composed entirely of cellulose, it features an interconnected network of nanoscale fibres, abundant hydroxyl groups, and a water content exceeding 95%. Its tunable thickness and adaptable mechanical and rheological properties make it a promising platform for developing next-generation material formats.

Key Features & Properties

  • High Water Content: More than 95% water, providing a hydrated, hydrogel-like structure.
  • High Purity: Composed of cellulose, a polymer of repeating glucose units rich in hydroxyl functional groups.
  • Tunable Thickness: Customizable from a few micrometres to several millimetres.
  • Nanofibrous Architecture: Initial hydrogel nanofibres typically range from 20–60 nm in diameter, with larger apparent fibre dimensions possible at greater thicknesses due to aggregation.
  • Mechanical Flexibility: Can withstand tensile strains of up to 30%.
  • Adaptable Flow Behaviour: Thin films can undergo plastic deformation. The rheological flow point varies from approximately 0.5 to 20 Pa, depending on thickness.
  • Thermal Stability: Reported to remain stable at temperatures up to 200°C under the conditions evaluated.

Designed for Diverse Applications

Its high water content, nanoscale fibrous network, functional hydroxyl groups, and tunable physical properties offer opportunities for applications in wound care, tissue engineering, active-ingredient delivery, personal care formulations, and functional material development.

Dried Sheets & Composites

Engineered Porosity. Customizable Structure. Versatile Performance.

Bacterial Cellulose Dried Sheets are lightweight, cellulose-based materials whose density, porosity, and rehydration behaviour can be tailored through controlled drying methods. Depending on the drying process, the sheets can be engineered as dense, compact structures or highly porous, reswellable networks, enabling versatility across healthcare, personal care, packaging, filtration, and advanced material applications.

Key Features & Properties

  • Customizable Microstructure: Sheet density and porosity can be tailored by selecting the appropriate drying method.
  • Oven-Dried Sheets: Form dense, compact structures with reduced porosity.
  • Freeze-Dried Sheets: Retain a highly porous network with the ability to reabsorb water and reswell.
  • Cellulose-Based Composition: Derived from bacterial cellulose, with a nanofibrous architecture and abundant hydroxyl functional groups.
  • Application-Driven Design: Drying conditions can be selected to achieve the desired balance of compactness, porosity, and rehydration performance.

Potential Applications

Suitable for exploring applications in wound-care materials, absorbent products, active-ingredient delivery, filtration, sustainable packaging, and functional cellulose-based composites.

Patterned Bacterial Cellulose

Engineered Topography. Nature-Inspired Functionality.

Patterned Bacterial Cellulose combines the inherent advantages of a nanofibrous cellulose network with customizable surface architectures. Through imprinting techniques and patterning during bacterial cellulose production, a variety of surface features, geometries, and feature sizes can be created to meet specific application requirements.

By integrating controlled surface topography with the flexibility of bacterial cellulose processing, Rekrite enables the development of functional material platforms for applications where surface structure, spatial organization, and microscale features play a critical role.

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Versatile Patterning Methods: Surface patterns can be introduced through imprinting or during bacterial cellulose production.
  • Customizable Feature Size: A range of feature dimensions, geometries, and surface architectures can be explored.
  • Nanofibrous Cellulose Network: Combines nanoscale fibrous structure with engineered surface features.
  • Application-Specific Design: Patterns can be tailored to suit functional, biological, or microstructured material requirements.

Potential Applications

  • Templates and Replication: Patterned substrates for transferring surface features to other materials.
  • Security Paper: Surface-textured cellulose materials for exploring distinctive patterns and authentication features.
  • Microfluidics: Structured substrates for developing microscale fluid-handling platforms.

Bacterial Cellulose Porous Beads

Fermentation-Formed. Highly Hydrated. Naturally Structured.

Bacterial Cellulose Porous Beads are spherical, porous cellulose-based structures produced directly during fermentation. Featuring a cellular internal architecture and exceptional water-holding capacity, these beads can absorb and retain water at levels exceeding 100 times their own dry weight. With sizes ranging from 0.5 mm to 5 mm, they offer a versatile platform for applications requiring highly hydrated, porous, and functional biomaterials.

Key Features & Properties

  • Fermentation-Based Production: Beads form during bacterial cellulose fermentation.
  • Exceptional Water-Holding Capacity: Can hold water exceeding 100 times their dry weight under suitable conditions.
  • Cellular Architecture: Features a porous internal structure that can be explored for fluid retention and material loading.
  • Customizable Size Range: Bead diameters from 0.5 mm to 5 mm.
  • Cellulose-Based Composition: Combines a hydrated, porous structure with the inherent functional chemistry of cellulose.

Potential Applications

  • Agriculture: Water-retention materials and carriers for nutrients or beneficial actives.
  • Personal Care: Functional beads for exploring hydration and controlled delivery in formulations.
  • Healthcare: Research platforms for fluid management and biomaterial development.
  • Active-Ingredient Delivery: Porous structures for investigating the loading and release of selected compounds.

Bacterial Cellulose-Based Nanocellulose Slurries

Nanoscale Cellulose. Functional Chemistry. Endless Possibilities.

Rekrite's nanocellulose slurries are aqueous suspensions based on cellulose nanofibres (CNFs) and cellulose nanocrystals (CNCs), designed as versatile building blocks for advanced material development. Leveraging the abundant hydroxyl groups of cellulose, these materials can be functionalised and processed using selected acids, oxidising agents, and deep eutectic solvents to introduce diverse chemical functionalities and tailor material performance.

From surface modification to the development of functional nanocomposites, these slurries provide a flexible starting point for creating application-specific, cellulose-based solutions.

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Nanocellulose Building Blocks: Available as cellulose nanofibre- and cellulose nanocrystal-based aqueous suspensions.
  • Water-Based Formulation: Suspended in water for versatile processing and material development.
  • Customisable Functionalisation: Chemical modification using suitable acids, oxidising agents, and deep eutectic solvents.
  • Rich Surface Chemistry: Abundant hydroxyl groups offer opportunities to introduce a wide range of functional groups.
  • Flexible Processing: Adaptable starting materials for developing coatings, films, composites, gels, and other functional architectures.

Potential Applications

  • Coatings and Films: Functional surface layers and nanocellulose-based films.
  • Advanced Composites: Reinforcement and functionalisation of polymer matrices.
  • Healthcare and Personal Care: Development of functional carriers and material platforms.
  • Packaging: Cellulose-based coatings and composite materials.
  • Environmental Applications: Exploration of functional nanocellulose for adsorption, separation, and filtration.
