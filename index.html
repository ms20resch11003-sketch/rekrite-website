BC Hydrogel

High-Purity, Water-Rich Nanofibrous Gel

A translucent to white, uniform gel engineered for advanced applications. It features a robust nanofibrous architecture with an exceptional water content exceeding 99%.

  • Source Organism: Derived from Komagataeibacter Hansenii ATCC 53582.
  • Nanofibrous Architecture: Fiber diameters ranging from 50–100 nm.
  • Mechanical Strength: Hydrated tensile strength between 1 and 5 MPa.

BC Hydrogel Scientific Data

Technical Specifications

  • Dimensions (L x W): Customizable up to a maximum of 45x30 cm.
  • Thickness: Ranges from 2mm to 15mm.
  • Water Content: ≥ 99% at a stable pH of 7.
  • Sterility & Storage: Autoclaved and supplied in sterile water, acetic acid, or preservative.

BC Dried Sheets

Engineered Porosity & Strength

Versatile cellulose sheets available in freeze-dried and oven-dried formats, offering highly tunable microstructures and porosity for targeted industrial applications.

  • Freeze-Dried: Spongy, paper-like opaque sheets with ~98% porosity.
  • Oven-Dried: Transparent to opaque sheets with a dense ~28% porosity.
  • Durability: Exceptional tensile strength ranging from 80 to 150 MPa.

BC Dried Sheets Scientific Data

Technical Specifications

  • Source Organism: Komagataeibacter Hansenii ATCC 53582.
  • Crystallinity Index: 85% -XRD.
  • Freeze-Dried Fiber Diameter: 10nm to 200nm.
  • Dimensions: Standard sheet sizes of 30x40 cm (freeze-dried) and 45x30 cm (oven-dried).

Patterned Bacterial Cellulose

Engineered Topography. Nature-Inspired.

Combines a nanofibrous network with customizable surface architectures via imprinting. Ideal for applications requiring spatial organization and microscale features.

  • Custom Geometries: Tailored feature sizes and patterns.
  • Templates & Replication: Transfer surface features seamlessly.
  • Microfluidics: Structured substrates for precision fluid handling.

Patterned Bacterial Cellulose

Engineered Topography. Nature-Inspired Functionality.

Patterned Bacterial Cellulose combines the inherent advantages of a nanofibrous cellulose network with customizable surface architectures. Through imprinting techniques and patterning during bacterial cellulose production, a variety of surface features, geometries, and feature sizes can be created to meet specific application requirements.

Potential Applications

  • Templates and Replication: Patterned substrates for transferring surface features to other materials.
  • Security Paper: Surface-textured cellulose materials for exploring distinctive patterns and authentication features.
  • Cell Growth: Topographical cues to influence cell attachment, alignment, and organization.

BC Beads (Xpearls)

Hydrated Spherical Carriers

Translucent to white spherical structures offering unique physical forms including spiked and irregular variations, maintained in an aqueous suspension.

  • Source Organism: Komagataeibacter Hansenii ATCC 53582.
  • Customizable Size: Bead diameters range precisely from 0.5 mm to 5 mm.
  • Environment: Maintained in a water suspending medium at a neutral pH of 7. 8].

BC Beads (Xpearls) Scientific Data

BC Slurry / Suspension

CNF and CNC Nanoscale Dispersions

Transparent to milky white aqueous suspensions of nanoscale cellulose, providing high stability with zero sedimentation and requiring no preservatives.

  • Nanofibrils (CNF): Features an aspect ratio of 70 to 500, homogenized via high shear blending.
  • Nanocrystals (CNC): Features an aspect ratio of 35 to 40, isolated via chemical synthesis.
  • Tunable Density: Solids content ranging from 0.5 to 10 % w/w.

Nanocellulose Slurry Scientific Data

CNF Specifications

  • Rheology: Shear-thinning.
  • Zeta Potential: -7 to -23 mV.
  • Homogenization Method: High shear blending.

CNC Specifications

  • Rheology: Shear-thinning.
  • Zeta Potential: -34 to -42 mV.
  • Synthesis Method: Chemical.
